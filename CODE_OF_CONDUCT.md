# Code of conduct

## Scope

This code applies to every project space: issues, pull requests, commit discussions and any channel where someone represents the project.

## Expected behaviour

- Discuss the work, not the person. Criticism must be about code, design or documentation.
- Assume good faith and ask for clarification before escalating.
- Keep reports factual: version, command, output, log excerpt.
- Respect privacy. Do not publish another person's server address, credentials, certificate material or customer data.
- Accept a decision when a maintainer explains it, even when you disagree, and raise it again with new evidence rather than repeating the same argument.

## Unacceptable behaviour

- Harassment, insults, threats or personal attacks.
- Discrimination or exclusionary remarks about any group or individual.
- Spam, unsolicited advertising or repeated off-topic posts.
- Publishing private information without permission.
- Deliberately submitting code designed to damage a system, exfiltrate data or bypass the project's security controls.
- Impersonating a maintainer or another contributor.

## Reporting

Report a violation to **@DatPHP**. Include what happened, where, when and any relevant links. Reports are handled confidentially; the reporter is not identified without consent.

## Enforcement

Depending on severity and history, a maintainer may:

1. ask for the behaviour to stop and remove the offending content;
2. issue a formal warning;
3. temporarily restrict participation;
4. permanently ban the account from project spaces.

A decision is recorded with its reason. A person affected by a decision may ask for a review by replying to the same contact once; the review is handled by a maintainer who was not involved in the original decision.

Security reports follow SECURITY.md instead of this document.
