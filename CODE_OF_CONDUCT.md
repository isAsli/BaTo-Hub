# Code of Conduct

Participants in BaToHub are expected to behave professionally and respectfully.

## Rules

- Be respectful to other contributors and users.
- Do not harassment, insult, or threaten anyone.
- Do not discriminate on the basis of identity, background, or belief.
- Do not spam, troll, or disrupt discussions.
- Keep discussions focused on the project.

## Reporting

Report conduct issues to @DatPHP.

## Enforcement

Reports are reviewed and handled according to the severity of the behavior. The goal is a safe and constructive project environment.
